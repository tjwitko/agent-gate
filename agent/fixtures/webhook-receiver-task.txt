Build and deploy a webhook receiver for our payment provider's event notifications.

WHAT IT NEEDS TO DO
- Accept HTTP callbacks from the provider at a public endpoint.
- Establish that each callback genuinely came from the provider before acting on it.
- Keep every callback received, so that a chargeback dispute raised weeks later
  can be settled by what we actually received at the time.
- Let our support team look up past callbacks by the provider's event id.
- Expose a health endpoint for the load balancer.

REQUIREMENTS
- Once a callback has been recorded, its contents must stay exactly as received.
  Nothing in the running system should be able to change or remove a record.
- Only our support team should be able to read past callbacks. The provider is
  the only party that should be able to submit them.
- The provider rotates its signing secret every quarter.
- Records are retained for seven years.

DEPLOYMENT
- AWS, single region.
- Terraform for all infrastructure.
- Kubernetes manifests for the service.
- A Dockerfile for the container image.
